Set your double-corrupt cost

A Temple double corrupt (Locus of Corruption) isn't free — it costs currency to run. Enter what it costs you so the Double EV columns aren't over-optimistic. You can change it anytime in ⚙ Settings.

Cost model: buy a level 1 / 0q gem, level it yourself (free), use GCPs for quality. Corruption targets a +1 level gem (21 for standard gems). EV/attempt already accounts for the resale of failed corruptions. Double-click any price cell to set a manual override; clear it to go back to the API price. All settings & overrides are saved in your browser.
